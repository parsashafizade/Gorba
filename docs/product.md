# Product notes

Tiny Yes is intentionally cute, theatrical, and obviously fictional. Each scenario follows four
visual stages: ask, scenario choice, final input, and a dedicated celebratory result. The shared
joke is that every real click on No makes No less convenient and Yes more charming.

Exactly two locales are supported: Persian (`fa`, RTL) and English (`en`, LTR). Language preference
is the only persisted state.
